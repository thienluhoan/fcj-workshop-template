### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**
The AWS office at Bitexco is professional and quiet, with clear rules (check-in times, dress code, work areas). That helped me build habits like working at a real company. Office days also helped me focus better than studying at home.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**
Mentors and the admin team answered very quickly in the group chat, even questions about procedures, office registration and check-in. Their feedback on team architectures was very practical, for example "number the paths and describe each step" and "add a network layer and WAF". I applied this feedback directly to our team's diagram.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**
I study Information Assurance, so Cloud Security and DevSecOps fit very well. I applied concepts like least privilege, defense in depth, identity management, vulnerability scanning and auditing to a real system on AWS, not just in theory.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**
The First Cloud Journey workshop library is huge, and its step-by-step guides are easy for beginners to follow. Beyond technical skills (Terraform, CI/CD, AWS security), I learned to collaborate through pull requests, write technical blog posts and present an architecture with cost figures.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**
The intern community is large and active. Teams share their architectures in the group chat for everyone to comment on, and every question gets an answer. The atmosphere is relaxed but competitive in a good way, pushing everyone to build better projects.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**
We could attend many high-quality events for free, such as AWS Cloud and AI Day, a Fireside Chat with Dr. Werner Vogels, the Buildathon and community meetups. That is a rare opportunity for students. Office days are flexible and booked per day on the portal.

### Additional Questions

**Overall satisfaction:** Fairly satisfied. The program met my expectations for learning AWS systematically and building a real project.

**What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**
Designing and deploying a complete system on AWS myself: from understanding the problem, choosing the architecture and estimating cost, through security and CI/CD. Meeting inspiring people like Dr. Werner Vogels in person was a bonus.

**What do you think the company should improve for future interns?**
I don't have any major suggestions for the program at the moment.

**If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why or why not?**
Yes. The AWS Study Group / FCAJ community is huge and someone is always willing to help. The program offers many learning opportunities: a free workshop library, practice credits, professional events and direct mentor feedback on your project.

### Suggestions & Expectations

**Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**
No specific suggestions. I hope the program keeps its architecture review sessions and community events as they are.

**Would you like to continue this program in the future?**
Yes. I would like to stay in the community, share my experience with future cohorts and pursue AWS certifications (Solutions Architect – Associate, Security – Specialty).

**Any other comments:**
Thank you to the mentors, the admin team and my teammates for supporting me throughout the internship.
